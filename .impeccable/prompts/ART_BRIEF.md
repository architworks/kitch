# Kitch art brief

Drop each finished file into `frontend/public/art/` with the exact filename below. The app already references these paths and falls back to `countertop.png` (heroes) or no illustration (spots) until a file exists.

**Style for every image:** attach `frontend/public/countertop.png` as the style reference. Warm painterly storybook illustration, soft flat shading, gentle light, same brushwork as the reference. Palette: olive and forest green, warm wood, turmeric orange, steel grey, cream. **No people, no text or lettering, no logos.**

---

## 1. Hero illustrations — 3 images, landscape 1536 × 1024, opaque PNG

Shown behind "Good morning / afternoon / evening, <name>" and tonight's meal. Text sits on the left, so **the left 42% must be an empty, smooth deep olive-green wall (#3f4229), softly vignetted, nothing on it.** The kitchen lives on the right side: light wooden counter, cream tiled wall, small window. Keep the three images as the *same kitchen* in different light.

**`hero-morning.png`**
> Indian home kitchen, early morning. Soft pale-gold sunlight through the window across the counter. On the counter: a steel saucepan of chai with a small glass of chai beside it, a plate of poha with peanuts and a lemon wedge, a potted tulsi plant, a steel masala dabba with its lid off showing round katoris of turmeric, red chilli and mustard seeds, a few bananas.

**`hero-afternoon.png`**
> Same kitchen, bright midday daylight. On the counter: a stainless-steel pressure cooker, a pot of yellow dal, a bowl of steamed rice, a stacked steel tiffin carrier, a chopping board with tomatoes, green chillies and coriander, a small brass bowl of ghee.

**`hero-evening.png`**
> Same kitchen, evening. Window shows a dusky indigo sky; a warm pendant lamp glows over the counter with cozy amber light. On the counter and a two-burner stove: a kadai of gently steaming aloo gobi, a stainless-steel pressure cooker, a rolling board with a few rotis and a rolling pin, a round steel masala dabba with turmeric, red chilli and cumin, a whole cauliflower and potatoes, a sprig of coriander.

## 2. Sign-in scene — landscape 1536 × 1024, opaque PNG

**`signin.png`**
> Same kitchen and style, late afternoon. The **left 45%** is the same empty deep olive wall (a sign-in card sits there). On the right: a woven jute grocery bag spilling fresh vegetables (tomatoes, spinach, okra, onions) beside a steel thali set for a meal, a stack of steel katoris, a potted plant, warm sunlight on the counter.
